How a session runs
  1. Listen plays the master's theka with the master's own early and late timing.
  2. Learn starts with a check cycle: four count-in clicks, then you play 16 beats with no help. Your unaided score sets the guidance: the better you play, the fewer cues. Every fourth cycle is another check cycle.
  3. Cues arrive just before each stroke: a phone vibration (one pulse for the dayan, two for both hands), a glowing pad, and the master's sound, which gets quieter as guidance falls.
  4. Test, device off runs four silent cycles after a count-in. This is the retention score for the pilot.

Master A and B are example profiles built for this demo, not recordings of real masters. Replace them with a recorded guru's fingerprint.
