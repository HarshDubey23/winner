## Do not present these as completed

- A manufactured wearable or full-sleeve electronics assembly.
- Measured sensor accuracy, actuator latency, battery life, comfort or safety.
- Tabla-recognition accuracy from the electronic-drum dataset.
- A measured improvement in learner retention.
- A confirmed artisan partnership, endorsement or participant study.

## Highest-value missing proof

1. One physical channel: ESP32 + IMU + haptic driver + vibration motor.
2. A measured latency table from motion event to vibration onset.
3. One consenting tabla teacher reviewing the cue concept and correcting the exercise design.
4. A short, recorded user test comparing unguided, fixed-cue and fading-cue practice.
5. A realistic bill of materials, target price, battery estimate and sleeve comfort constraints.
