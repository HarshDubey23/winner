## Narration and reuse

Narration is synthetic, generated with Microsoft Edge hi-IN-MadhurNeural at its normal rate. It is not a human voice recording. No playback time compression is applied. The script is original. No generated cultural photograph is used in this edit.

The bilingual jury cut also uses Microsoft Edge en-IN-PrabhatNeural for selected English passages. Its low-volume documentary underscore is an original programmatic composition made for this film. The Kathputli and loom cues are folk-inspired instrumental textures; they are not presented as an authentic traditional song or field recording. Real ambience from the credited cultural footage remains in the mix.
