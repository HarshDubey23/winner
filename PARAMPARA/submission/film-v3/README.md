# PARAMPARA — performance-led four-minute film

Watch `PARAMPARA-4min-Jury-Film.mp4`. It runs 4:10 and begins with a restrained ten-second jury ident. English subtitles are burned in; `PARAMPARA-Jury-English.srt` is also included. The Hindi/Hinglish narration is synthetic and runs at natural speed. It is not a human recording.

## What changed

- Real moving tabla, Kathputli and handloom footage replaces the repeated photographic montage.
- Real performance ambience remains softly audible under narration, with six short tabla chapter accents.
- The opening establishes the project as a digital prototype and research demonstration before the story begins.
- The problem is framed around feedback during solo practice between lessons.
- Licensed live tabla audio punctuates the edit; source speech is muted in the explanatory tabla footage.
- The original sleeve CAD and selected component images remain consistent.
- Evidence and future plans are distinguished explicitly. No new hardware accuracy or human-study result is claimed.

## Evidence boundaries

The wearable is unbuilt. CAD animation is a geometry demonstration, not a validated physics simulation. Software checks cover generated inputs. The public drum-data analyses concern electronic drums, not tabla proficiency. Kathputli and weaving are proposed extensions. The film’s learning problem is a design motivation, not a completed field-study finding.

The 1,000 scoring cases, 425 timing/fading checks and 27 existing host checks refer to earlier executed repository evidence; this edit does not create additional test results. The public-data figures are reproduced analyses described in the research dossier. They do not prove improved learning.

## Reproduce or record a human replacement

`prepare.py` writes the script. `make_voice.py` generates the approved synthetic narration. `build_film.py` builds the cut from the source footage and earlier CAD clips; dependencies are Python, Pillow, NumPy, imageio-ffmpeg and edge-tts. The source workspace needs the adjacent film-v2 folder for CAD and evidence assets. No human narrator was recorded.

The separate Voiceover-Hinglish.wav and Performance-Soundtrack.wav are editable stems. VOICEOVER-SCRIPT.md includes cue times for a future human recording. The share package is a delivery package, not a standalone renderer environment.
